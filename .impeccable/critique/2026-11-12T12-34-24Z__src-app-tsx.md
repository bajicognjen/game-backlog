## Overall Impression

The web surfaces are now in good shape: fair at the high-stakes moments, measurably accessible, nothing breaks from 300 to 1280 px. What remains is depth: the conflict asks for a choice between totals, a first-time user with an invite code is nudged to the wrong door, and a phone gets the whole desktop Sync toolkit at once. The biggest opportunity is showing the games that differ in the conflict, the one moment a plan can be lost.

## What's Working

- **The conflict dialog is fair.** No primary choice, newer time bold, seconds on a shared minute, both columns at 320 px, choices stacked full width with Decide later last, text as the dialog's description, an Undo with a pausable clock.
- **Consequence-first copy.** Each destructive control's effect sits under it; a missing and a wrong invite code read differently; "no plan under this key" says what to check.
- **Joining by link is nearly one tap.** Key filled and stripped from the address bar, focus on Connect; Turn off is immediate, reassures, and has an Undo that waits while focused.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] A focused sync result can sit under the sticky bar or below the dialog's fold**
- Why it matters: after Delete server copy at 375 the focused confirmation scrolls under the 52 px top bar (`.settings-group-body > .banner` has no scroll-margin, unlike `#sync-settings`); in the conflict dialog at 320×568 the autofocused "Decide later" is below the fold with the heading scrolled off. WCAG 2.4.11.
- Fix: `scroll-margin-top: calc(var(--topbar-h) + 12px)` on banners in Sync (or all banners); in Modal, `scrollIntoView({ block: "nearest" })` on the focused control.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] The conflict choice is made on totals, not on the games that differ**
- Why it matters: four aggregates (`SyncConflictDialog.tsx:98-121`); two devices that each added a different game look identical but for the time; contradicts "made from evidence, not on trust"; after 20 s the replaced plan is gone as far as the UI says.
- Fix: "Only on this device: Hollow Knight, Portal 2 (+1 more)" / "Only on the server: …" by title; a sentence naming the safety net (daily backup, or "Export this device's plan first"); the outcome paragraph cut to two sentences.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P1] A new user's first device is nudged toward Join**
- Why it matters: on an empty copy Join is primary (`SyncSettings.tsx:239-244`) and the empty plan's only button (`App.tsx:393-397`), but a brand-new user's first device is empty too and must start; the invite code shows only after choosing Start; "Start on the device that has your plan" does not fit someone with no plan yet.
- Fix: fork on what the person holds ("Got an invite code? Start syncing here" / "Already use it on another device? Join with its link or key"), equal weight on an empty copy unless a join link opened the form; keep the hours-per-week sentence in the empty hint.
- Suggested command: /impeccable onboard

**[P2] The phone progress bar has 0 height (polish regression), and 600–899 px with a mouse gets the phone layout**
- Why it matters: the column `.now-progress` plus the base `.progress { flex: 1 }` resolves the bar's height to 0 (321×0 at 375, 546×0 at 600), contradicting DESIGN.md; the hours text still shows. At 600–899 with a mouse the card shows an ~800 px Finish bar and three-line rows though the table fits from 900.
- Fix: `.now-progress .progress { flex: none; }` in the phone block; scope the two-column action grid to `(pointer: coarse)` or ≤600 px.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P2] On a phone Sync is a pile of buttons, and Change opens far from its trigger**
- Why it matters: 6 equal buttons when synced, 8 with Change open; key, link and QR stay visible in "Different plans" and "Server copy deleted" where they lead nowhere; Change opens Turn off and Delete 242–416 px below itself, below the fold, without scrolling; a phone shows a QR for another camera and offers no Share.
- Fix: render the Change options under the status row; hide sharing in gone and conflict; on a phone lead with "Share join link" (`navigator.share`, falling back to copy), key and QR behind "Other ways"; QR on wide screens only.
- Suggested command: /impeccable distill

## Minor Observations

- The delete dialog's button says only "Delete"; nothing says it cannot be undone or that shared join links stop working.
- After Turn off or Delete, a blue "Join a plan from another device" sits under a blue Undo and suggests joining right after a deliberate stop.
- "Open Sync" focuses "Start syncing again", a re-upload; two quick Enters re-create a deliberately deleted copy.
- Short phone notices wrap to two lines (200 px message basis): "Plan updated from the server." is 115 px tall.
