## Overall Impression

The scariest moment from last time, the conflict, is now the best-designed screen in the web app. What remains is what happens around it: where results appear, and how a second device finds its way in. The biggest opportunity is making the second device's first two minutes obvious; today the friend can follow every hint and still end up with two plans.

## What's Working

- **The conflict ledger.** Tabular, right-aligned counts (28 vs 3, 11 vs 0), the newer time in bold, neither choice styled as primary, focus on "Decide later", which on a phone sits last where the thumb ends up; Escape returns focus to the opener.
- **The Folded Ledger at 320–899 px.** Three lines per game on one left edge, explicit table roles, 40 px targets on touch, denser rows with a mouse; at 600 it reads as the same product, not a fallback.
- **Sync state language and its errors.** A word, a dot, the time since, one line of meaning; invite missing vs refused told apart; a key with nothing behind it says what to do next; focus returns carefully after every action.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The conflict Undo and sync notices land off-screen when you act from Settings**
- Why it matters: the Undo is the safety net for the one choice that replaces a whole plan. Chosen from Settings on a 375 phone, the notice renders 1,024 px above the viewport and `Banner.tsx:40` focuses it with `preventScroll: true`; the user only sees "Synced just now". "Plan updated from the server" after joining is hidden the same way.
- Fix: when the action starts in Sync, show the result and its Undo (with the clock) inline under the sync state; otherwise scroll the notice into view (`block: "nearest"`) when it is off-screen.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] The friend's second device is led into a separate plan**
- Why it matters: every invited user meets this. On a fresh phone the empty plan says nothing about sync; Sync sits under IGDB and Steam behind an icon-only button; the off state leads with the invite field and a primary "Turn on sync", so someone holding an invite code makes a new key with an empty plan. On a device with games, Connect lands on a red "Different plans" state instead of asking (`platform/sync.ts:362`).
- Fix: in the off state ask first, "Start syncing from this device" vs "Connect to a plan on another device", with the invite field only on the first; on an empty plan make Connect primary and add a sync line to the empty state; open the conflict dialog right after Connect when the plans differ; fold unconfigured IGDB and Steam in the web build.
- Suggested command: /impeccable onboard

**[P2] The conflict dialog clips its Server column at 320 px**
- Why it matters: on the smallest phones the evidence the choice depends on is cut behind a horizontal scrollbar. The UA `dialog:modal` max-width beats `width: calc(100vw - 16px)` (app.css:3229), and the row labels are `nowrap` (app.css:2257). It fits at 360 and 375.
- Fix: `max-width: none` on `.modal` below 900 px; let the labels wrap under ~360; cell left padding 16 → 8 px; stack date over time.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P2] Sync failures stay inside Settings and speak in internals**
- Why it matters: "Sync failed" and "Not syncing" (too large) never reach the main page, so a device can stop syncing for days unnoticed; what people do read is "That didn't work (network)" / "(other)" and "HTTP 500". After a server-copy delete, other devices get "This server needs an invite code", or on an open server quietly re-create the copy, contradicting the delete warning.
- Fix: a quiet banner or top-bar dot for stopped states (not Waiting); a plain sentence with a recovery for each SyncError reason; on a missing copy after a delete: "The server copy was deleted. Turn sync on again or turn it off."
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P2] Screen readers hear noise in one place and nothing in another**
- Why it matters: the `role="status"` region wraps a relative time that a 30 s ticker updates (SyncSettings.tsx:50-54, 210-217), so it is re-announced every minute while Settings is open; key errors (:188-192) have no role and focus returns to Connect, so they are never read; the invite field's name swallows its hint and error.
- Fix: keep the time out of the live region; focus the field or use `role="alert"` on a key error; move the hint out of the `<label>` and keep `aria-describedby`.
- Suggested command: /impeccable audit

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (phone, one thumb):** the only route to Sync is the top-right sliders icon plus ~1,400 px of scroll; after a conflict choice from Settings the Undo is out of sight; "Finish game" wraps alone in the Now playing card at 320 and 375; the progress bar shrinks to a ~40 px stub at 320; the 32-character key is typed by hand with no tolerance for 0, 1 or 8; tapped buttons keep their hover tint (Done, Copy, Check now).

**Sam (keyboard / screen reader):** minute-by-minute status announcements; "No plan is stored under this key" never read; focus goes to the off-screen Undo, and Dismiss sends it to the Now playing link far above; after Delete server copy, focus lands on primary "Turn on sync", so a stray Enter makes a new key; the idle card's "Start" does not name the game.

**The friend (has the web URL and an invite code, sets up a phone and a PC):** the empty plan never says "connect to your plan from another device"; the invite field and primary button lead to a second key; on a device with games, Connect gives a red state with no question; just after joining the status reads "Synced 9 min ago"; Turn off on the only synced device loses the key with no confirmation, orphaning the server copy.

## Questions to Consider

- If the sync key is the only identity, why does it travel by hand? A join link or QR with the key in the URL fragment (never sent to the server) would make the friend's setup one tap.
- On a web copy with an empty plan, should the app lead with "Connect to your plan" rather than IGDB and Steam forms the phone may never need?
- Is a conflict an error (red in Settings) or a decision (blue banner)? Should anyone ever see it without the question right after pressing Connect?
- When both copies hold the same games, should the app skip asking altogether?
