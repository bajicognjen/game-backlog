## Overall Impression

First-run P1s (overflow, keyboard reach, dark contrast) are gone; new issues are refinements of what now exists. The finish moment is the peak; its faults are a short fuse and focus landing on the next game's Finish. Biggest opportunity: Finished games reads as a to-do list.

3. Keyboard and focus discipline with AA contrast throughout.

[P1] After a finish, focus sits on the next game's "Finish game" button. Fix: move focus to the notice (tabindex -1) so Undo is next; return focus to the card when it closes. Command: harden.
[P2] Finished games reads as a to-do list: muted "Rate" link for unrated, large numeral for rated, merge Review + Note, covers at poster ratio. Command: quieter.

[P2] Two date formats in one dialog (native picker locale vs dd.mm.yyyy). Fix: lang="en-GB" on the document. Command: polish.
