## Priority Issues

**[P1] The friend's invite code is refused on a phone**
- Why it matters: the invite input (`SyncSettings.tsx:289-300`) has no `autoCapitalize`/`autoCorrect` off, and the server compares exactly (`worker/sync.ts:86`), so iOS sends "Review-invite" and the friend's first sync fails with a message that blames the code.
- Fix: `autoCapitalize="none" autoCorrect="off"` on the field, a case-insensitive compare on the server, and a mention of capitals in the refusal.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] Focused notices vanish after 8 s and leave the page jumping**
- Why it matters: "Sync is on…", "Joined…", "Deleted…" take focus and clear themselves after 8 s (`store.ts:108`, `202-209`) without pausing; focus drops to `<body>` (both reviewers reproduced it); on a phone every Sync control shifts ~110 px; the notices carry instructions. They are `role=status` regions holding Dismiss/Undo, so their button labels are read, and turning sync on or off also updates the empty plan's status region at once.
- Fix: notices placed in Sync stay until dismissed or the next action (or pause on hover/focus like Undo); on removal focus goes to the group's first control; the message lives in a hidden text-only status region, as the banners already do.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] After "Server copy deleted" the copy contradicts itself and the way out leans one way**
- Why it matters: Turn off's hint says "The server copy stays…"; "Start syncing again" is the only promoted action and opens straight into a red "needs an invite code" error; Cancel there turns sync off unsaid; starting again revives the same key and old links though the delete dialog says "This can't be undone… join links… stop working".
- Fix: two equal choices with hints ("Start again from this device" / "Stop syncing here"); Turn off's hint for this state; ask for the invite calmly before sending, not as an error; restart under a new key or make the delete warning true.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P2] The conflict dialog opens scrolled past its question on small phones and stretches with a mouse**
- Why it matters: `data-autofocus` on "Decide later" (`SyncConflictDialog.tsx:90`), which CSS orders last; at 320×568 the dialog opens scrolled 203 px (title at y −174, ledger hidden, focus ring clipped at the bottom); at 375×667 the title is cut by 2 px. DOM order Decide later, Keep, Take against visual Keep, Take, Decide later. At 600–899 with a mouse the `.modal` full-width rule makes the ledger ~860 px with full-width bars.
- Fix: DOM order = visual order; focus the dialog (or Decide later with preventScroll) and keep scrollTop 0; full width and stacked actions only below 600 px or on coarse pointers, otherwise 560 px.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P2] Sync says things the screen doesn't show**
- Why it matters: "Sync is on. … scan the code below" (`en.ts:373`) with no code shown in either layout since distill (this round's regression); the add-device hint names a QR that a phone keeps behind Other ways; "Check now" is identical on success and failure (with `/api/sync` failing the status still read "Synced 2 min ago": a failed check with nothing unsent goes idle, `platform/sync.ts:322-327`); "too big" still offers Check now.
- Fix: notice and hint written around what is on screen; after a manual check "Checked just now, up to date" or "Couldn't reach the sync server…" announced once; Export in place of Check now when the plan is too big.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (phone, one thumb):** 8 s notices shift the page under the thumb; at 375×667 the desktop short-window rule `@media (max-height: 700px)` (`app.css:3015`) hides Now playing's status word, Design chip and note (phones with Safari's bars are often under 700 px tall); the conflict dialog opens past its title; the finished-games rating pill is 42×26 with no hit-area extension; the Now playing link hit areas overlap; Settings, Open Sync and Choose which to keep sit at the top.

**Sam (keyboard / screen reader):** focus drops to `<body>` when a focused notice expires; the conflict dialog's tab order is not its visual order; notice regions read their button labels and two regions speak when sync turns on or off; a join link on an already-synced device is neither focused nor announced; Check now is silent; plan tabs read "PC26", "Together2".

**The friend (web URL + invite code, phone and PC):** a capitalised invite code is refused; phone setup ends on "scan the code below" with no code; "Credentials stay on each device" is jargon to someone without IGDB or Steam; turning off on their only device orphans the server copy unwarned; "Ask whoever sent you the link" assumes a link.

## Questions to Consider

- Is the invite code a server secret or a word friends pass along? If the latter, case-insensitive, or carried inside the link?
- Should a synced plan show its state on the main page, so Settings → Sync is rarely visited?
- On iOS with the PWA installed, does a scanned QR open in Safari (separate storage) instead of the home-screen app?
