## Priority Issues

**[P1] Sync health is invisible outside Settings**
- Why it matters: synced, off and waiting look identical on the main page; only a stop or a conflict surfaces. The friend opening the phone after setting up the PC sees an empty plan with no sign it is synced. The phone top bar gives three of six slots to the theme switch and none to sync.
- Fix: a compact sync glyph with the state dot in the top bar, opening Sync (on a phone it takes the theme switch's place; the theme moves into Settings). A synced empty plan says games from the other device appear here. The conflict banner takes Sync's "Different plans" treatment, not the reload banner's.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P1] Re-joining with an identical plan forces the conflict dialog, which then says something false**
- Why it matters: `platform/sync.ts:386-391` treats any non-empty local plan as a conflict; `SyncConflictDialog.tsx:156` then says they "differ in hours, order or what is finished" when nothing differs. It teaches that the scariest dialog is noise and invites a wrong click.
- Fix: compare content on join; join silently when equal; when only timestamps differ, say "These plans match" with one button.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] Check now is silent when sync has failed, and the check note goes stale**
- Why it matters: in "Sync failed" Check now makes a request and shows nothing (`SyncSettings.tsx:232` returns unless idle); offline, green "Synced just now" sits beside "Couldn't reach"; "Checked just now" never ages and contradicts "Synced 1 min ago" beside it. Breaks PRODUCT.md's "a manual check says what it found".
- Fix: always say what the check found ("Tried again at 15:12: still no answer") with a time that ages or clears; show the last sync time in failure states; amber dot while the last check failed.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P2] The Undo clock runs when it shouldn't**
- Why it matters: it keeps counting while focus is on the notice box itself (only its buttons pause it) and behind the Delete dialog; a turn-off Undo expired while the reviewer was on it. At 1024 "17 s" wraps (`.banner-count` lacks `white-space: nowrap`). The time limit is hidden from screen readers.
- Fix: pause while focus is anywhere in the notice and while a modal is open; keep the count on one line; say the window once in words.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] Narrow layouts: a stretched phone page with a mouse, squeezed titles at 320**
- Why it matters: 600–899 with a mouse is the phone fold, then a jump to the full table at 900; at 320 the title column is 72 px (mid-word breaks); a two-line finished title's tap area overlaps Rate; at 320×640 the conflict choices sit below the dialog fold with a 6 px sliver as the scroll cue.
- Fix: a two-line row for 600–899 with a fine pointer; at ≤340 move row actions under the title; stop the title's tap area above Rate; a bottom fade or pinned choices in the conflict dialog on short phones.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**Sam (keyboard / screen reader):** Check now in a failed state is silent; the Undo clock runs while focus is on the notice, and its limit is never announced; two announcements at once after Undo; "Add another device" is a plain span, not a group label; row buttons don't name their game.
