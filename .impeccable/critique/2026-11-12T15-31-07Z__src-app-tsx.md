## Priority Issues

**[P1] Copies that aren't syncing say they are**
- Why it matters: `App.tsx:317` shows "This plan syncs" for any non-off state (gone, failing, invite-required); invite-required has no status (`syncStatus.ts` default), so the top bar reads "Sync:" with a ring and no banner; on load a saved copy shows green "Synced" until the first check; a successful check doesn't refresh "Synced 2 h ago". Breaks "a check that can't get through never reads as Synced"; the friend thinks the phone syncs.
- Fix: derive the empty-plan line from the status; give invite-required an amber "Not set up yet" status, a banner and "Finish setting up"; show "Checking…" (quiet) until the first check; refresh lastSyncedAt on every successful check.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] An offline first start loses the invite code, then scolds**
- Why it matters: `enable()` sends the code only with its first push and the form clears it; back online the server refuses the new key and a red "needs an invite code" appears over an empty field though the code was typed; meanwhile Add another device offers a link for a key the server doesn't have.
- Fix: keep the code with the meta and send it with every push at revision 0 until stored; hold Add another device back until the first sync, with a line saying why.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] Joining has no payoff; a join link on an already-syncing device is silent**
- Why it matters: "Joined…" appears inside Settings while the arrived plan stays covered; a link on a syncing device gives a muted hint, no focus, nothing announced, and says "another plan" even for a dead key.
- Fix: "Joined: 26 planned and 11 finished games arrived" with "Show the plan" (closes Settings); on a syncing device, a focused notice with "Switch to this plan" (Turn off + Connect, so the conflict dialog still guards the choice).
- Suggested command: /impeccable onboard

**[P2] Focus and announcement leaks**
- Why it matters: closing Settings with an Undo pending moves the notice to the top and takes focus off-screen; the reload banner's Later and dismissing a top notice on an empty plan drop focus to body; after Undo of Turn off two live regions speak; a notice back in Sync within 8 s expires under the user (~150 px shift); the first 500 says "again".
- Fix: a notice that moves to the top doesn't take focus; Later and Dismiss hand focus on (the plan's first control when there's no current game); Undo of Turn off announces once; a notice back in Sync waits again; "again" only after a failure.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] A small phone's first screen is crowded**
- Why it matters: at 375×667 sync + reload banners take ~330 px; at 320×568 Now playing's title sits at ~440 px; an empty plan shows "Plan ends –"/"Hours left 0"; Sync notices put a lone Dismiss on its own row; Sync with Change and Other ways open shows 9 buttons.
- Fix: hide the tiles on an empty plan; a one-line reload offer on phones; a corner Dismiss in notices on phones; Change and Other ways close each other.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout
